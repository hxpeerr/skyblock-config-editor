**SkyBlock Config Manager** finds settings that overlap between Fabric SkyBlock mods and keeps each feature switched on in only the mod you choose.

### Download
- **`SkyblockConfigManager.exe`**: 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. One file, nothing to install. **`SkyblockConfigManager.zip`** is the same file, zipped, in case your browser blocks the .exe.
- **`SkyblockConfigManager-macOS.zip`**: macOS 10.13 or newer, Apple and Intel chips. Unzip it and open the app.
- **`SkyblockConfigManager-Linux.zip`**: 64-bit Linux. Unzip it and run `./SkyblockConfigManager`.
- **`skyblock_config_manager.py`**: the Python version, for any system with Python 3.8 or newer.

Put the app in your `.minecraft` folder (or your launcher's instance folder) and open it; it finds the `config` folder by itself, and backs up each config as a `.bak_*` file before it's first saved.

The apps aren't code-signed. On Windows, the first time you run it, "Windows protected your PC" may show up: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. On macOS, right-click the app and choose **Open**, or click **Open Anyway** in **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.
